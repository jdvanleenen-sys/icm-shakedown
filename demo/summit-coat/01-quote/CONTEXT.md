# 01 Write a quote

## Inputs
- The site visit notes, in the job's folder under `jobs/`
- The rates in `pricing/rates-2026.md`
- The voice in `voice.md`

## What to do
Measure from the notes, price each line from the rates file, add GST, and write the quote in
the template's shape. Keep the whole quote under 120 words, so the customer reads it on a phone.

## Example
Hi Priya,

Thanks so much for having us out on Tuesday to look at the house, it was great to meet you and
the dog. We had a good look at everything and here is what we would recommend for the work you
asked about. The living room and hallway walls come to 640 square feet, so at $2.50 a square
foot that is $1,600. The trim around the windows and doors is 180 linear feet at $1.90, which is
$342. That brings the subtotal to $1,942, plus GST of $97.10, for a total of $2,039.10. We can
usually start within 2 weeks of a yes, and the job takes about 3 days. If you have any questions
at all, or want to change anything, just let us know and we will sort it out.

Thanks,
Dana at Summit Coat

## Human check
The owner reads every quote and checks each price against the rates file before it goes out.
